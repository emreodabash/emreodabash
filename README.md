# Hi, I'm Emre 👋

I'm a **mechanical engineering student** interested in how ideas turn into things that actually work.

I'm exploring different parts of engineering before deciding where I want to go deeper. Some days that means mechanics and design; other days it's electronics, code, or figuring out why a prototype isn't behaving as expected.

I'm learning, building, and keeping track of what I discover along the way.

### What I'm exploring

- **Mechanical engineering** — design, mechanisms, materials, and manufacturing
- **Robotics & autonomous systems** — how machines sense, move, and make decisions
- **Electronics & embedded systems** — circuits, microcontrollers, and hardware–software integration
- **Control & programming** — using code and mathematical models to understand and improve systems
- **Prototyping** — turning an idea into something testable, then improving it

I'm not trying to call all of these my specialties. I'm trying to find out which questions I want to spend more time answering.

### Currently

- Studying the fundamentals of mechanical engineering.
- Practicing **C** and **Python**, and learning electronics and PCB design with **KiCad**.
- Taking part in a student rocketry project, including work on its electronics.
- Exploring projects in robotics, automation, and practical engineering.
- Making time to document the process, not just the finished result.

I've also been involved in technology education and volunteer activities. Working with other people has been just as valuable as working with tools.
